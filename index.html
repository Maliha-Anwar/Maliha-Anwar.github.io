About me

Researcher at heart, community in focus

I'm a public health researcher at the University of Michigan's Youth Violence Prevention Center, where I lead qualitative and mixed-methods research, program evaluation, and research communications across CDC-funded initiatives. With an MPH in Health Policy from The George Washington University and a background spanning global health at the Gates Foundation to community-level violence prevention, I bring a cross-sector perspective to health equity work.

🔬

Research Focus

Youth violence prevention, socioeconomic determinants of health, and community-engaged research. My work bridges multiple sites and communities — from youth advisory boards in Muskegon and Washington, D.C. to county-level health data across 3,000 U.S. counties.

📊

Analytical Approach

I work across both quantitative and qualitative methods — from regression modeling and data visualization in R to thematic analysis of youth narratives and community interviews. I believe the strongest insights come from combining the two.

🌱

Currently Building

Expanding my data analysis portfolio with projects in R, Quarto, and Shiny. Developing research communications that translate complex findings for broader audiences. Exploring doctoral study for Fall 2027.

💡

Why It Matters

Health outcomes are shaped by the systems and communities people live in. I want to make that visible — through data, through community voice, and through research that informs more equitable solutions.

County Health Rankings: Socioeconomic Factors & Health Outcomes

Explored how county-level socioeconomic factors like income, insurance coverage, education, and unemployment predict premature mortality across ~3,000 U.S. counties using the 2025 County Health Rankings data. Built a full analysis pipeline in R covering data wrangling, exploratory visualization, multiple linear regression, and reproducible reporting with Quarto.

● In Progress

Advancing Youth Leadership in Community-Level Violence Prevention

Analyzed survey data from youth advisory boards across five CDC-funded Youth Violence Prevention Centers to identify factors that drive youth leadership. Using a sample of 151 youth across six U.S. cities, findings showed that leadership efficacy and authentic adult support—particularly recognition and receptivity to youth voice—were strongly associated with increased leadership behaviors. This work highlights the critical role of youth-adult partnerships in building effective, community-driven violence prevention strategies.

A Case Study of Scattered-site Illegal Dumping and Its Effects on Vacant Property Greening in Two U.S. Legacy Cities

Analyzed the impact of illegal dumping on greening efforts across two U.S. legacy cities using qualitative interviews with residents and stakeholders. Identified key patterns linking physical disorder, resource constraints, and reduced community capacity. Generated insights to inform scalable, place-based interventions that support environmental health and equity.

Critical Analysis of Socio-cultural Factors and Community-Based Interventions: Reducing Child Mortality Due to Diarrheal Diseases in Pakistan

Conducted a critical analysis of the socio-cultural, economic, and environmental factors driving child mortality due to diarrheal diseases in Pakistan. Through a systematic review of global literature, identified key barriers that limit the effectiveness of evidence-based interventions, including cultural norms, resource constraints, and gaps in community engagement. Findings highlight the importance of tailoring public health strategies to local contexts to improve intervention uptake, sustainability, and health outcomes.

Writing & publications

Stories that matter

Quantitative research is essential for understanding patterns and trends, but it doesn't capture the full picture. Qualitative insights — the stories, experiences, and perspectives of the communities most affected — add a powerful layer of understanding that numbers alone can't provide. My work spans both spaces, and my writing includes research communications, blog series, and upcoming publications, with current work focused on youth-led violence prevention through the University of Michigan's Youth Violence Prevention Center.
